# Local-Champion Kit (P3 growth)

One page per town launches the network: a poster on a wall, a WhatsApp group,
and a 5-minute training. No ad spend — paper + groups + word of mouth.

## 1. Poster copy (A4, print in EN or FR)

> **EAGLE EYE AFRICA — [Town name]**
> Lost something? Found something? Selling? Warning your neighbours?
> Post it FREE in 1 minute — no account needed.
> [QR to /{locale}/notices] · eagleeyeafrica.org
>
> FR: **EAGLE EYE AFRICA — [Ville]**
> Objet perdu ? Trouvé ? À vendre ? Alerte pour vos voisins ?
> Publiez GRATUITEMENT en 1 minute — sans compte.
> [QR vers /{locale}/notices] · eagleeyeafrica.org

QR target: always the **Notices** page (Lost & Found is the killer job), never
the homepage. Generate with any QR tool pointed at
`https://eagleeyeafrica.org/en/notices` (or `/fr/notices`).

## 2. WhatsApp group template

- **Name:** `Eagle Eye — [Town]` / `Eagle Eye — [Ville]`
- **Description:** `Lost & found, alerts, market. Post on the site, share the
  link here. No advance payments — meet in public. / Objets perdus, alertes,
  marché. Publiez sur le site, partagez le lien ici. Jamais d'avance —
  retrouvez-vous en public.`
- **Pinned message:** the poster text above + the 3 hero links
  (`/submit/notice?type=lost_found`, `?type=missing_person`,
  `?type=community_alert`).
- **Rules (post weekly):** no forwards from outside the town, no MoMo
  advances, corrections welcome — reporting an error visibly fixes the story.

## 3. Five-minute training script (for the champion)

1. (1 min) Open `/notices` on your phone. Tap a hero card — show the 3-tap
   Lost flow ending at the receipt (`Ref #…`).
2. (1 min) Tap the mic button — dictate instead of typing. Show it works in
   French too.
3. (1 min) Open a listing — tap reveal contact, then point at the safety tip:
   never pay a deposit up front.
4. (1 min) Share a story to WhatsApp with the share button — show the
   branded preview card.
5. (1 min) "Your job: one post per week from this town, and forward every
   published story into this group."

## 4. Pilot scoreboard (review weekly)

- Time-to-first-post (guest, 3G): < 90 s, completion > 60%.
- Weekly active communities (places with ≥ 1 publish): +5/wk.
- WhatsApp shares/post > 3; scam reports/listing < 1%.
- Moderation latency median < 4 h; queue zero overnight.

Start with **3 pilot towns**. A town graduates when it posts weekly for a
month — then its champion trains the next town.
